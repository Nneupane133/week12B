============================================================
OVERALL CONCLUSION
============================================================

ONE REASSORTANT VIRUS is likely if:
  ✓ Cross-strain pair rate in AL isolate > 5%
  ✓ Different segments assigned to different strains (S1133 vs DIV)
  ✓ Cross-strain pairs share the same segment in R1 and R2
    (indicating within-segment recombination, not just co-packaging)

TWO SEPARATE VIRUSES are likely if:
  ✓ Cross-strain pair rate < 1% (noise level)
  ✓ All 10 segments map cleanly to ONE strain only
  ✓ No cross-strain pairs share the same segment

IMPLICATION:
  If ONE reassortant → original paper may have assembled a
    single virus's natural genetic diversity into 'two strains'.
    Plaque purification failed because there was only one virus.

  If TWO separate → original paper's conclusion is correct,
    and the follow-up question becomes: why did purification fail?
